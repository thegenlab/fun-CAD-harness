Design and build a portable folding stool. Two leg frames cross in an X and are
joined by a central pivot shaft; a seat (fabric strap or slats) spans the two top
rails. Opened, the stool must stand on the floor without tipping and carry an
800 N seated load on the seat for 2 s with the seat sinking less than 10 mm and no
leg sliding apart. A stop must keep the legs from opening past the working angle.
Folding the legs about the pivot must close the stool to under 80 mm total
thickness, and unfolding must return it to the same open, loaded-ready shape. All
parts must be printable.
