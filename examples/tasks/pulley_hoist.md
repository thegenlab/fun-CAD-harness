Design and build a two-pulley block and tackle mounted on a frame. A fixed
upper pulley and a moving lower pulley carry a cord; the load hangs from the
lower pulley. Pulling the free end of the cord down must raise a 1 kg load, and
the pulling force needed to lift it steadily must be close to half of the load
weight (a 2:1 mechanical advantage, allowing for friction). Pulling the cord by
100 mm must raise the load by about 50 mm. Model the cord with an allowed
flexible or equivalent representation if a fully detailed rope is too costly.
