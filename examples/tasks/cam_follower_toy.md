Design and build a hand-cranked cam toy. Turning the crank rotates an eccentric
or pear-shaped cam on a shaft supported by a frame; the cam lifts a vertical
follower rod through a guide, and gravity returns the follower so it stays in
contact with the cam. One full crank revolution must raise the follower by at
least 15 mm and lower it back to its starting height, and three consecutive
revolutions must repeat the same lift without the follower jamming in its guide
or leaving the cam. All parts must be printable and the follower must not be
fixed to the cam.
