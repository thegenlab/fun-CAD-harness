Design and build a small hand-operated screw jack. A handle turns a lead screw
that runs through a threaded nut in a base; the screw raises a load platform
that is kept from rotating by a guide. Turning the handle must lift a 2 kg load
on the platform by at least 20 mm, and the platform must hold its height when
the handle is released (the screw must not back-drive under the load).
Reversing the handle must lower the load again.
